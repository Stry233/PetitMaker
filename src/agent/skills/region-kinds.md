REGION KINDS: what each district or place is, chosen before any object. Players think in regions, never in single objects: a region is a kind of place, and trees, flowers, paving, water and hills are only what it is built from.

Choose kinds that differ, no two neighbours from the same group, and build each as what it is:
- views: a lookout on the highest ground facing the sea; a panorama terrace; a waterside deck or lakeside walk; a picture or a word drawn in water and land, read from above;
- nature: a central park; a flower garden of beds; a flower sea in broad bands; a sunken bamboo court; a lake; a tree avenue or tree grid; a landscape of hills and falls alone; a canyon cut between two high terraces with water at its floor;
- culture and play: a stage raised high and facing the moon or the sea; a giant board game laid in flight prints; a sports field laid in field prints;
- growing: crop plots of tilled soil; an orchard of one fruit per block;
- homes: each neighbour's house a small region of its own, never house against house, dressed for its owner; load neighbors for who lives in each house and what they love;
- anchored: the shop and the gallery on the main route from the station.

Taste (understand, do not copy): a home backed by a hill and a waterfall with its front left open; a stage on high ground facing the moon; a sunken bamboo court; the shop ringed by a one-level terrace with another place on top of it.
