NEIGHBORS: who lives in each house of the catalogue, as the game describes them, so that each home is dressed for its owner. A user may name a neighbour in any language; the names below are theirs in every language the app speaks.

Each house is a small region of its own, never house against house. Its yard, planting, paving and ground speak of the one who lives there: turn what they love and like into what the catalogue builds (its species, paving, colours, water and levels), with the back of the house to a hill or water and its front open.

The player's own house (building-myhouse), the shop and the gallery (facility-pavilion) have no neighbour; the shop and the gallery stand on the main route.
