# RECIPES (proven legal patterns — adapt sizes freely)
- Ground lake: water needs no caps at elevation 0 when surrounded by flat ground at the same level — paint_terrain water elev 0 with a circle just works on open grass. For a LOWER neighbor situation (lake at a cliff edge), cap the exposed faces with mountains at elev 0+1.
- Elevated pond: 1) paint_terrain mountain elev N as a filled shape; 2) paint_terrain water elev N on INTERIOR cells only (keep a >=1-cell mountain ring — use a smaller shape, or outline:true for the rim first). The ring at exactly elev N contains the water.
- Waterfall: you do NOT draw water flowing down. A waterfall is ONE high water cell at a cliff edge; the game animates the fall automatically. You never paint a stream, line, or river of water going down the slope — that is the single most common mistake and it always reverts. The falling cell STAYS at the pool elevation N. Proven sequence (verified rule-legal):
  1) Build the high ground at elevation N (sculpt_terrace or paint_terrain mountain).
  2) Build the LANDING FIRST: the cells directly in front of where it will drop must ALREADY be one uniform lower elevation across the full width (a flat shelf at N-1, a ground pool, or plain ground). Nothing falls into a stepped slope — that is the V-WTR-03 revert.
  3) Pool: paint_terrain water elev N on interior cells with a mountain ring at elev N (contained pond).
  4) The lip: paint_terrain water elev N on just 1-3 front-edge cells that have mountain at EXACTLY elev N on both perpendicular sides (the frame) and the uniform lower landing directly in front. Done — the fall renders itself.
  Minimal legal unit: a `mountain(N) water(N) mountain(N)` strip at the cliff row, with a uniform lower shelf in the row in front of it. For a taller drop, step it: one uniform shelf per level, never a diagonal water column.
- Hill with access: paint_terrain mountain elev 1 (rect/circle), then use find_ramp_sites to locate valid cliff-edge anchors and the matching ramp item, then place_object at a returned anchor — the heightDrop snap orients the ramp automatically. For elev 2+, build terraces (each tier >= 3 cells wider than the one above for V-MTN-03 headroom at >=4) and call find_ramp_sites again for each tier.
- River + bridge: paint_terrain water elev 0 as a STRAIGHT line (width 3-6) across grass — uniform width, flat equal banks. Then find_bridge_sites → place_object at a returned anchor. Round ponds usually have NO legal crossing (uneven span); give a pond a straight narrow waist if it needs a bridge.
- Road: build_road lays a whole path in one call (line or cells); roads need flat terrain under every cell.
- Forest/meadow: find_flat_areas for the centerpiece (house/facility), place it, then scatter_objects with 2-4 species of trees or flowers around it (rect a bit larger than the clearing).
- Village block: find_flat_areas with the building's footprint (see CATALOG), place_object the buildings a few cells apart, then connect doors with a road: roads are 1x1 items — place_object one road cell at a time along the path (straight runs look best), and dress edges with scatter_objects flora.
