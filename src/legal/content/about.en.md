# About {app}

{app} is an unofficial map-planning tool made by players for *Petit Planet*. Arrange terrain, water, roads, objects and annotations in your browser, explore your ideas, then build them in the game.

The editor checks the building rules it implements to help identify layout problems. Game versions and actual building conditions may differ from the editor; use the game itself as the final reference.

## Using and Saving Your Work

The tool is free, with browser-local storage rather than user accounts or cloud saves. For long-term storage or editing on another device, export a JSON save.

Procedural and on-device model styles run on your device. The Agent and online illustration features use AI services you connect yourself, with charges determined by the provider. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for data flows and the [Terms of Use](/terms) for use and sharing rules.
