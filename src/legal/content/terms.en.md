## 7. Exports, Sharing and Recoverable Data

JSON saves contain map data. The Planning annotations option is enabled by default; turning it off excludes annotation data. Depending on your selections, these files may also include notes, generation details, provenance, undo history and editor state. A file with undo history may retain earlier content that is no longer visible on the current map.

When Importability is enabled and code generation succeeds, a share image includes a PetitGlyph map code. Anyone holding the image may recover its map and annotations, including annotations hidden from the picture. Show notes affects the rendered picture only. To share the picture alone, choose Plain image or turn off Importability.

A plain image still shows its visible text and designs. Choose recipients and publication settings based on the information the file actually contains. Sharing a file with recoverable map data does not itself grant the recipient a copyright license to adapt or distribute the work. You may separately specify permission to adapt or republish your work.

Export creates a file on your device; it does not automatically publish a work to this project. Uploading, editing or forwarding that file on another platform uses that platform's service.
