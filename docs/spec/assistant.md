# Assistant

This area covers the AI assistant that edits the open map on the user's instructions: how it connects to the user's own provider and protects the key, how its edits undo and roll back, how a painted region and the map rules bound it, how a job is paused, stopped and steered, and how approvals and text screening gate its work.

## Providers and keys

- **ASST-01** The assistant runs on the user's own provider account: the user brings a key for a built-in provider or a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint, and requests go straight from the browser to that provider, never through a PetitMaker server.
- **ASST-02** Telling which provider a key belongs to happens on the device without any network request; an ambiguous key needs the user's choice, and a key is sent only to the provider or endpoint the user selected.
- **ASST-03** A saved key stays on the device, sealed under a key the browser will not export where the browser supports it and otherwise kept only in obfuscated form, never as plain text.
- **ASST-04** A key never appears in the assistant's record: provider error text is cleared of the live key and of anything shaped like a key before it is shown or stored.
- **ASST-05** Removing the key of the provider a job is running on stops that job first.
- **ASST-06** A custom endpoint must use HTTPS, except a loopback address on the user's own machine, which may use HTTP; a plain-HTTP address elsewhere is upgraded to HTTPS, and credentials written into the address are removed.

## Undo and rollback

- **ASST-07** Each edit the assistant makes is one undo step.
- **ASST-08** The assistant's own undo never reaches below the point where its current job started, so it cannot take back work from before the job; each step it undoes is one map edit, taken back with any selection steps made after it.
- **ASST-09** Rewinding a job asks first and says how many steps it will take back, including how many of them are the user's own later work.
- **ASST-36** A job is rewound only on the map it was made on and only while no job is running: a record made on another map, or one that does not say which map it was made on, offers no rewind; while a job runs every rewind stands disabled and says why; and a rewind that would take nothing back is not offered. A press that reaches a refused rewind by any other way changes nothing on the map.
- **ASST-10** When map rules refuse part of an assistant edit, only as much is rolled back as the map needs to be legal again, and the panel says in the user's language what was refused and which changes stayed; an edit that fails partway is rolled back whole.

## Painted region

- **ASST-11** While the user has a region painted for the assistant, every assistant edit must stay inside it, judged by whole object footprints; an edit that strays is refused, the whole call is rolled back, and the panel shows that the work was kept to the region. Generate's scope and a blueprint's frame are no such region. The panel's region button paints the assistant's own region and lights only while that brush paints; it never ends Generate's or Blueprint's step. Choosing a mode ends its painting but keeps its region and the undo of its strokes, which the region button picks up again, and the map shows the assistant's region while its panel is open and no region step or Generate stands in front of it.
- **ASST-37** When the assistant raises ground or lays water over an area that reaches the shore, it lays every part the rules allow, as a shape does (EDIT-02): ground beside the shore that cannot take raised land or water is left out and the rest is laid, and the edit is never refused whole for it.

## Running a job

- **ASST-12** The user can pause a job at any moment, mid-reply or between steps, and resume it from where it stopped, and a job interrupted by a reload comes back paused. Stop ends a job at once, after which the assistant makes no further requests or edits for it.
- **ASST-13** A message sent while the assistant works steers the running job instead of starting a second one.
- **ASST-26** A job works on the map that was open when it started: opening another map, a planet or a restored map stops it as Stop does, its stop card saying that another map was opened, and the pictures it takes show that map, never what the editor shows in its place.
- **ASST-14** A job finishes on the model and endpoint it started with; a change of model or endpoint applies from the next order.
- **ASST-30** A helper edits only inside the area the assistant gave it, which never reaches outside the region the user painted, and cannot undo. Helpers the assistant briefs in one turn work at the same time, their edits reaching the map one at a time. The panel shows each working helper's progress under the step that started it. An area larger than one place goes to a lead of its own, which plans it and briefs helpers for its places; that lead hands out places only. When it ends, the assistant is told whether the helper finished, finished with some edits refused, ran out of turns or failed, and what the area gained or lost; an area that misses the painted region or the map starts no helper.

## Approvals and screening

- **ASST-15** The user chooses how often the assistant asks: Strict asks before every change, Checkpoint asks at plan steps and wide changes, and Autopilot builds without asking; a change of level applies from the next edit.
- **ASST-16** Under Checkpoint, undoing, redoing, clearing an area and handing work to a helper ask for approval every time, even after the plan was approved.
- **ASST-17** Every approval describes the action in plain language in the user's language, never as raw data or internal names, and the user can allow or decline it.
- **ASST-31** When the user asks, the assistant can operate the app outside the map: change the language, the interface size, motion, the graphics quality, the cursor, the keyboard shortcut style, the view, the grid, chunk bounds and layer numbers, zoom, fit and turn the map view, show, hide and lock layers, show or hide the annotations and the interface, open the app's windows, turn What's this on or off, show the welcome tips, enter or leave immersive mode where the browser grants it, close or dock its own panel and choose the side it docks at, and set the choices of a share picture or a save file, the picture's footer line, adding or removing the shots of its 3D preview, and choosing or removing one of the session's illustration takes among them, and open the export window on them. It never starts a download: the user reads the export window and confirms there. A job that did what was asked through an app action, or answered from Help (ASST-32), ends once it answers, without being sent back to edit the map. It cannot erase local data, reset the keyboard shortcuts, delete a planet, a blueprint or a blueprint folder, or run a command that edits the map, its selection or its history. Source: owner ruling 2026-11-18.
- **ASST-32** Asked how to do something in the app, the assistant looks it up in the app's Help and in the key bindings in force, the user's own changes included, and answers from what they say. It reads Help as the user's edition, language and input mode show it, with the keys the user's keymap binds, and looking either up asks for no approval.
- **ASST-33** An app action asks for approval by the same levels as an edit: one that only reads never asks, one that changes the app asks under Strict alone, and one that leaves the page or is hard to take back asks under Strict and Checkpoint; Autopilot asks for none. An app action is no edit of the map: it adds no undo step and is not counted among the job's edits. Source: owner ruling 2026-11-18.
- **ASST-34** When the user asks, the assistant can organise the blueprint library and the saved planets: list the folders and blueprints, rename a blueprint, move blueprints into a folder or out of every folder, reorder them, create or rename a folder, and export blueprints or a whole folder as a file, which is written only once the user has read and accepted the export notice it opens, and save the frame the user has drawn and confirmed in Blueprint mode as a blueprint, under the name, author and folder asked for, while the save step is open (BLUE-05); list the planets, rename a saved planet, and move the map to another planet with its build; and open the Import window, where the user chooses the file. A name the assistant gives passes the same name check as a typed one. Switching planets and exporting blueprints each ask for approval as an action that is hard to take back. It deletes nothing: blueprints to delete are moved into a Pending Deletion folder, which the user deletes, and it never opens a planet empty in the open map's place. Source: owner ruling 2026-11-19.
- **ASST-18** Before the model first reads an order, the order passes the same on-device text check as exports, and a refused order ends at once with a local reply and is never sent to the provider.
- **ASST-19** Notes and typed answers sent during a job pass the same check, and a refused one is dropped while the job carries on; when the check cannot run, orders, notes and answers go through.

## How the assistant builds

- **ASST-20** The assistant has no tool that runs the generator; it builds through its own editing tools, so every change is a step the user can see, approve and undo. Source: owner ruling 2026-08-27.
- **ASST-35** The assistant gives and reads every position as a cell of the grid objects stand on. Terrain it paints, erases or shapes for a set of cells is the terrain those cells rest on, so water or raised ground given the same rectangle as a block of objects is centred where the block is, the nearest cells that take an object lie the same distance out on every side, a platform takes objects on exactly the rectangle it was raised for, and a region it reads, painted again as read, is unchanged.
- **ASST-39** When the assistant looks at the map to judge its work, it sees the map as built, without its plan annotations drawn over it unless it asks for the plan, and it can see the ground alone, each level in its own colour and every waterfall marked.
- **ASST-40** The assistant and the helpers it briefs can carry water down terraces in waterfalls, each fall framed as the rules ask (V-WTR-02, V-WTR-03); a river begun inside a raised pool falls at each step it crosses.
- **ASST-41** Asked to build a home for one of the game's neighbours, named in any of the app's languages, the assistant knows which house is theirs and what the game says they love. Source: owner request 2026-11-20.

## Plans on the map

- **ASST-21** For a layout across several areas, a whole map, or an order that asks for a plan, the assistant lays its plan out as planning annotations before building: tagged and coloured zones for the spaces the order needs, sized in cells on the open buildable ground, entrance zones or routes for the way people move between them, and chips for focal points. It plans by a stated method: a programme with the instances and sizes each function needs, paths drawn first as a connected hierarchy reaching the plaza, the other fixed arrival points and every parcel, and parcels tiling the ground between the paths; its written plan is a short legend of those annotations. Under Strict and Checkpoint the plan's approval is asked with that layout standing on the map. Source: owner request 2026-10-29.
- **ASST-22** Annotations already on the map reach the assistant with each order as requirements, with their number, tag, size and place; a zone the assistant draws by shape leaves the cells other zones hold to those zones unless it is told to overlap them, and the assistant changes or removes annotations it did not lay for the current order only when the user asks. Source: owner request 2026-10-29.
- **ASST-23** The assistant's plan annotations stay on the map after it builds, as the record of the plan, until the user removes them or asks the assistant to; undoing its terrain work or rewinding its job leaves them, and the Annotations undo takes them back one edit at a time. While the Annotations layer is hidden or locked the assistant writes no annotations and gives its plan as text. Source: owner request 2026-10-29.
- **ASST-24** A custom tag the assistant names for a facility is drawn only after it passes the same on-device text check as a tag the user types. Source: owner request 2026-10-29.
- **ASST-27** Before a provider is connected, the panel offers two ways to connect, signing in first and an API key second. Signing in is with one provider: OpenRouter on the international site, ModelScope on the mainland China site. It opens the provider's own page in a window; once the user consents there, the panel connects with a key or token of the user's own account as if it had been pasted, and a note says that account is charged. A blocked window, a declined consent and a failed exchange are each said in that note, and a sign-in under way can be cancelled. Source: owner request 2026-11-17.
- **ASST-28** When the provider refuses a key that came from a sign-in, the error card offers Sign in again as its repair in place of fixing the key, and the composer says so; a refused key that was pasted asks for a key as before. Signing in again replaces the key without dropping it first, the card then says the sign-in is in place, and the refused instructions are sent again only when the user presses Try again. Source: owner ruling 2026-11-18.
- **ASST-29** When a provider refuses a request because its account is not completed there, such as an account not yet linked or an email address not yet verified, the error card says the account is not ready and offers Try again; the key is kept and no key is asked for. Source: owner ruling 2026-11-18.
- **ASST-38** A provider answer whose connection drops partway is a network fault: the turn is retried as a failed request is, and the job does not end for it.
- **ASST-25** Asked to build from annotations, its own approved plan or zones, chips and routes the user drew, the assistant builds each zone as its tag or name means with what the catalog offers and inside the zone's cells (its area tools take a zone's number and keep to it), lays roads along path zones and routes, leaves reserved zones empty and the annotations standing, reads an untagged zone or a free custom name from its context and asks only when the choice changes the result materially, and reports what it could not build and why. Its read of the annotations tells what each zone holds and which zones hold nothing. Source: owner request 2026-10-29.
